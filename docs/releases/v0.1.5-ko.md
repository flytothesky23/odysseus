# v0.1.5-ko

This patch adds a Korean management-analysis research genre to the Odysseus Korean fork.

## Highlights

- Adds a new Deep Research category: `management` / `경영분석`.
- Teaches the research loop to treat business, audit, financial, daily, weekly, monthly, and annual management reports differently from general articles.
- Adds category-specific instructions for planning, query generation, evidence extraction, synthesis, stopping criteria, and final report writing.
- Adds management-report output expectations:
  - 경영 요약 / Executive Brief
  - 손익, 비용, 자산·부채·자본, 현금흐름, 재무비율, 리스크
  - PSBall 판매 mix, 슬래그반출, 함안·청남 가치 회수, 물류/운임, 인력·장비 생산성
  - 종합의견 및 관리 Check Point
- Adds a management visual-report skin with KPI-board/table styling.
- Extends Korean E2E smoke coverage so the category appears in both chips and dropdowns.

## Why

Korean business users need Deep Research to produce operating reports, audit-style reports, and management dashboards from private Obsidian/local-source evidence, not only generic web research summaries.

The implementation was shaped by two local reference assets:

- A Korean audit/business-report PDF used as the ideal report tone and structure.
- A weekly report contract package that defined Korean terminology, PSBall/logistics/productivity report sections, and reader-facing wording rules.
