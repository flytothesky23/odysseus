# v0.1.5-ko

This patch adds a Korean management-analysis research genre to the Odysseus Korean fork.

## Highlights

- Adds a new Deep Research category: `management` / `경영분석`.
- Teaches the research loop to treat business, audit, financial, daily, weekly, monthly, and annual management reports differently from general articles.
- Adds category-specific instructions for planning, query generation, evidence extraction, synthesis, stopping criteria, and final report writing.
- Adds management-report output expectations for executive brief, finance, sales mix, logistics, productivity, and management checkpoints.
- Adds a management visual-report skin with KPI-board/table styling.

## Verification

- `venv/bin/python -m py_compile src/deep_research.py src/visual_report.py`
- `node --check static/js/research/panel.js && node --check scripts/e2e-ko-smoke.cjs`
- Targeted management category, visual report, date context, extraction controls, artifact export, and security regression tests.

## Known Gaps

- Full Python test suite and Docker runtime were not rerun for this patch.
- Live long-form management reports still depend on configured LLM/search/local-source quality.
