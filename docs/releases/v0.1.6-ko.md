# v0.1.6-ko

This patch lets Deep Research choose a reasoning effort per report run.

## Highlights

- Adds a `추론` selector to the Deep Research settings panel.
- Supports `자동`, `낮음`, `보통`, `높음`, and `매우 높음`.
- Sends the selected value as `reasoning_effort` through `/api/research/start`.
- Applies the normalized value to every Deep Research LLM call through `DeepResearcher._llm()`.
- Preserves the setting in active jobs, library entries, result metadata, session JSON, and Markdown frontmatter.
- Improves the Research settings grid so the new control wraps cleanly instead of squeezing the modal.
- Extends Korean E2E smoke coverage for the new selector and persistence behavior.

## Why

Deep Research is a multi-stage LLM workflow. Planning, query generation, evidence extraction, synthesis, stopping decisions, and final report writing can all benefit from an explicit reasoning-effort choice, especially for long Korean business or legal reports.

Use:

- `자동` for normal runs and provider defaults.
- `낮음` or `보통` for faster drafts.
- `높음` or `매우 높음` for important final reports where quality matters more than latency.
